Most popular routes

Five routes built around your chosen places.

Education · Local Life

European University of Lefke Route

A calm route through campus life, town streets, and scenic countryside views.

  • EUL Main Campus Entrance
  • Lefke Town Centre
  • Piri Mehmet Paşa Mosque
Practical info: Great for a half-day trip with easy parking and simple roads.
Insider tip: Early morning fruit stands near the town edge are a local favorite.

Sea · Food · Sunset

Aphrodite Beachfront Route

A soft coastal route focused on sea views, food, and the calm of sunset hours.

  • Aphrodite Beachfront Restaurant
  • Coastal Seafront Walk
  • Sunset Viewing Point
Practical info: Best enjoyed in the late afternoon when the light is at its best.
Insider tip: The side terrace seats usually give the best view.
Photo: sunset beach (images/beach.jpg)
History · Views

St. Hilarion Castle Route

A dramatic route through old stone landscapes and one of the region’s strongest landmarks.

  • Castle Entrance and Outer Walls
  • Historic Courtyard Area
  • Upper Terrace Views
Practical info: Wear comfortable shoes because the terrain can be uneven.
Insider tip: Early morning visits feel cooler and more magical.
Background: St. Hilarion (images/castle.jpg) — slightly blurred for atmosphere.
Leisure · Nightlife

Escape Beach Club Route

A more energetic coastal route built around beachside relaxation and evening atmosphere.

  • Escape Beach Club Entrance
  • Pool and Sunbed Zone
  • Sunset Deck Area
Practical info: Best for a late afternoon plan that continues into the evening.
Insider tip: Arriving just before sunset gives the best balance of light and space.
Photo: night drive / coastal road (images/road.jpg)
Harbour · History

Kyrenia Harbour Route

A lively waterfront route with boats, old streets, food, and a strong sense of place.

  • Harbour Quay
  • Marina Boats and Yachts
  • Historic Walls and Castle Area
Practical info: Great for walking and easy to explore in the late afternoon.
Insider tip: The smaller side streets often hide the best cafés.
